This week’s craft is…

My hand turkey

Find a scrap of paper and something to draw with. A pencil, pen or crayon will do.

  1. Spread one hand on the paper. Trace around it, then lift it up.
  2. Give your thumb an eye and a little triangle beak. Draw two feet below your palm.
  3. Your four fingers are the tail feathers. Make it your own! Try stripes, dots or a tiny hat.

A thank-you note for someone I love

Pick anyone you love. Grab a piece of paper and your favorite crayons.

  1. Fold your paper in half. Draw a little picture of something you love doing together.
  2. Think of one moment you want to thank them for. You can try your words in the journal below.
  3. Write your note inside, sign your name and give it to them.
