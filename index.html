My hand turkey

Find a scrap of paper and something to draw with. A pencil, pen or crayon will do.

  1. Spread one hand on the paper. Trace around it, then lift it up.
  2. Give your thumb an eye and a little triangle beak. Draw two feet below your palm.
  3. Your four fingers are the tail feathers. Make it your own! Try stripes, dots or a tiny hat.

A Thanksgiving leaf-rubbing card

Pick someone you’re thankful for. Find a leaf with bumpy veins, a sheet of paper, and a crayon with its wrapper peeled off.

  1. Lay the leaf bumpy side up under your paper. You can leave the sheet flat or fold it into a card.
  2. Rub the side of your crayon gently over the paper until the leaf appears. Take the leaf out and write “Happy Thanksgiving!” above your rubbing.
  3. Below the leaf or inside your card, finish “I’m thankful for you because…” with one little thing that person does. Draw the two of you together, sign your name, and give them your card.
